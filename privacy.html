FocusNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21

What data FocusNest accesses

FocusNest reads the URLs and titles of your open browser tabs to group them by domain and to save tab sessions at your request.

What data FocusNest stores

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage APIs:

  • Tab sessions — URLs and titles of tabs you explicitly save (never collected automatically)
  • Timer settings — your preferred work/break durations (Pro)
  • Blocklist — domains you choose to block during Focus mode (Pro)
  • Licence key — your Pro licence key (stored locally to verify Pro status)
  • Tab usage stats — a count of how often you open each domain (Pro; stored locally only)

Pro users who enable cross-device sync have their saved sessions synced via Chrome's sync storage — this uses Google's Chrome Sync infrastructure and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

What data FocusNest does NOT collect

  • FocusNest does not send any data to any server
  • FocusNest does not track your browsing history
  • FocusNest does not use analytics or telemetry
  • FocusNest does not require an account or login
