What it does

Three tools. One quiet toolbar icon.

See every tab at a glance

FocusNest sorts your open tabs into nests by website. Forty tabs become eight tidy rows — and the × next to each row closes the whole pile at once.

Stay in the zone

Start a 25-minute focus session with one click. The toolbar badge counts down the minutes, and short and long breaks schedule themselves after every session.

Block distractions while you work

Pro adds a site blocker that only runs during focus sessions — YouTube is blocked at minute 12 and back at break time. Plus custom timer lengths, weekly tab stats, and sessions synced across devices.
